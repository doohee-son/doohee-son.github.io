Doo-Hee Son

Post-Master's Researcher, Yonsei University

I am a Post-Master's Researcher in the Advanced Semiconductor Devices and Systems Lab at the School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Myung-Jae Lee.

My research focuses on CMOS single-photon avalanche diodes (SPADs): how junction doping-profile engineering can improve their performance, and how device structure can keep them stable under the gamma-ray doses they would meet in space. My work runs from Sentaurus TCAD simulation through chip design, with five SPAD tape-outs so far, to electrical and optical characterization and on-site gamma-ray irradiation tests.

I began SPAD research as an undergraduate researcher at the Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST) under the same advisor. I received my M.S. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (2026) and my B.S.E. in Nano Science and Engineering and in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (2024), both from Yonsei University, where I have also served as a teaching assistant for Physical Electronics.

Looking ahead, I am interested in single-photon detectors for space and biomedical applications, including superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors (SNSPDs).
