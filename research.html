Research

A single-photon avalanche diode (SPAD) turns one photon into a measurable avalanche current, which makes it useful wherever light is scarce and timing matters: ranging, biomedical imaging, quantum optics, and space instruments. I work on CMOS SPADs across the whole device cycle, from TCAD simulation and chip design to electrical and optical characterization and gamma-ray irradiation tests.

My current work asks two questions: how junction engineering can make CMOS SPADs faster and more efficient, and how device structure can keep them reliable under the ionizing radiation they would meet in space.

Radiation hardness of CMOS SPADs

2024 – present

I study how gamma-ray total ionizing dose (TID) degrades CMOS SPADs and which device structures resist it. Using Sentaurus TCAD models of oxide-trapped charge and interface traps, I showed that a silicide layer over the Si/SiO₂ interface suppresses TID-induced interface-trap generation in back-illuminated SPADs.

For on-site gamma-ray irradiation tests at KAERI Jeongeup, I built a portable multi-channel setup with custom readout PCBs and selective shielding that monitors the dark count rate (DCR) in real time. SPADs with a physical guard ring and a pinning layer showed a far smaller DCR increase than conventional SPADs. Four SPAD variants based on these results are now integrated into a 64 × 64 SPAD array chip for array-level tests.

In collaboration with Dr. Minwook Seong (Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute), Dr. Inyong Kwon (Yonsei University), and Prof. Byungchoul Park (Myongji University), who designed the array's readout circuitry.

SPAD performance in standard CMOS

2022 – present

For mobile applications, I engineered the junction doping of a front-illuminated CMOS SPAD to lower its breakdown voltage, improving timing resolution while preserving photon detection probability (PDP) and DCR. The work combines TCAD simulation of doping profiles and electric-field distributions with measurements of I–V, DCR, PDP, afterpulsing, timing jitter, and light emission, and builds on earlier back-end-of-line design studies of output contacts and metal layers.

A journal manuscript with EPFL and GLOBALFOUNDRIES Singapore is in submission to Optics Express.

SPAD chip design and tape-outs

2024 – present

I design SPAD test chips based on TCAD simulation, build the readout PCBs used to characterize them, and am currently developing an FPGA-based interface for chip-level measurements.
